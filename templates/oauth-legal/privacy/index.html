Privacy policy

This policy explains the limited identity information used to authenticate and authorize access to restricted resources.

Information received

When you authenticate with Google, the service may receive your Google account identifier, email address, name, and basic profile information made available by your account. The service never receives or stores your Google password.

Technical security records may include the time and outcome of an authentication attempt, source IP address, and browser information.

How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • Verify identity.
  • Apply access-control policies.
  • Maintain authenticated sessions.
  • Detect abuse and investigate security incidents.
A successful Google authentication does not by itself grant access. Authorization is evaluated separately.

Storage and retention

Authentication information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to operate and secure the service. Session information expires automatically. Security logs are kept for a limited period and are accessible only to the service administrator.

Sharing and disclosure

Personal information is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties. Limited disclosure may occur when required by law or when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, or maintain the security of the service.

Your controls

You can revoke the application's authorization from your Google Account permissions. You may request deletion of associated information by contacting the address below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect operational, legal, or security changes. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email privacy@your-domain.example.
